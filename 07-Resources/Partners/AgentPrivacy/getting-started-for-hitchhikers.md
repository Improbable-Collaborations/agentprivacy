## What Is This?

AgentPrivacy is the identity and privacy layer for the Fabulous Machine. It is the agentic expression of the First Person Project: once you have established that you are a real person, the dual-agent model ensures AI agents can act on your behalf without eroding that sovereignty.

The core idea: you have two agents working for you. One protects your boundaries (the Swordsman). One acts on your behalf in the world (the Mage). Neither can reconstruct your complete behavioral picture alone. That separation is the privacy guarantee.

---

## The 60-Second Version

1. **You own your data.** Not the platform, not the circle, not the AI. You.
2. **Two agents, one you.** The Swordsman holds your boundaries and keys. The Mage does things in the world on your behalf.
3. **Agreements are bilateral.** When you join a circle, both sides sign. No one-sided terms of service.
4. **Trust is earned, not declared.** Reputation builds through what others find when they look you up, not what you say about yourself.
5. **Zero-knowledge proofs** let you prove things ("I contributed 42 hours") without revealing things ("here's what I did minute by minute").
6. **Signed commits prove provenance.** Through Open Integrity (Blockchain Commons), every contribution has a cryptographic chain of trust back to its origin.
