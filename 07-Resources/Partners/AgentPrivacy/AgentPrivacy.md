## Overview

0xagentprivacy provides the identity, privacy, and agent-separation layer for the Fabulous Machine. Core thesis: **privacy is value** — behavioral data is a seventh form of capital currently being extracted by surveillance architectures. The dual-agent separation model (Swordsman ⚔️ / Mage 🧙) creates information-theoretic guarantees that prevent behavioral reconstruction through mathematics, not policy.

As the agentic expression of the First Person Project, agentprivacy turns first-person sovereignty into deployable agent architecture. The First Person establishes personhood. The dual-agent model ensures that personhood remains sovereign as AI agents act on your behalf.
